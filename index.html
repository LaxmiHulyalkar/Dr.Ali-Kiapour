Background

& Regulatory Science

I'm a Biomedical/Mechanical Engineer at KLS Martin Group where I use ANSYS Mechanical FEA to simulate four-point bending, torque, and structural behavior of surgical implants — corroborated by physical testing to ASTM standards. All simulation work is governed by the ASME V&V 40 framework, the same standard Dr. Kiapour serves on as an active committee member.

At the Arany Lab (SUNY Buffalo), I fabricated patient-specific prosthetics using SLA and DLP 3D printing and developed an ICP-based computational pipeline to validate anatomical fit across 34 patient samples. This fabrication-to-validation workflow — 3D printing → computational fit validation → statistical verification — directly mirrors Dr. Kiapour's work on 3D-printed implant biomechanics optimization.

Dr. Kiapour is the Director of Biomechanics Research at MGH / Harvard Medical School, with 20+ years of spine and orthopaedic biomechanics research, active ASME V&V 40 committee membership, and a focus on computational design optimization for 3D-printed orthopaedic implants. My background is a direct match.
